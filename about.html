About me.

Being a creative is at the core of who I am. I was born in Jamaica and moved to Florida where I completed my A.S. in Graphic Design at Broward College and B.A. in Web Design at UCF. My passion for creativity transcends into various facets of my life, and I enjoy utilizing it to tackle problems.

My expertise lies in graphic design and brand identity. Throughout my career, I've collaborated with a variety of businesses, honing a sharp eye for crafting distinct brand identities. A highlight of my work includes the comprehensive rebranding of Lifestones Gems and Minerals, where I was able to fully engage my skills in photography, graphic design, web design, development, and social media marketing.
